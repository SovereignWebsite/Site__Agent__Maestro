## Lessons for the protocol
1. restart.md should list "retire my old key entry in the Factory's team/keys.json (the conductor does it)", and the maestro asks for it as soon as the new key is published.
2. The maestro can re-seal from its own copy when it holds the vault file: seal only what the old session held, not more.
3. After a key retirement the site deploy may refuse for about 5 minutes (raw.githubusercontent.com cache); rerun the failed job, do not push again (observability.b, 196c896, live 22:30).
4. A session handover takes minutes when the area is current; the capture took 2 minutes because b.observability's files were already up to date.
