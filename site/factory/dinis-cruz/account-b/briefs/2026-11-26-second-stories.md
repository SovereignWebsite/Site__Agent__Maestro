# Brief: second stories, finding the gap behind each problem (26 Nov 2026)

Source: dinis.2026-11-26T1546Z.b. For b.stories, a new agent; budget with the accountant.

## What it is
Not a post-mortem. For each problem: what happened, what was missing, why the system let it happen (the second story), what lies under that (the third story), and the end-to-end fix, written as guidance where it belongs (a prompt, a ROLE file, a protocol, a tool).

## Examples to start with (proposed by maestro.b)
1. **"I couldn't find it on account B, so I asked the other maestros."** An agent should be able to navigate the estate itself, within a budget (for example 3, 5 or 7 hops) set by the workflow; failing within the budget means a gap in the map. Where is the map, and why could maestro.b not find Dinis's book agent?
2. **"I don't have the link back."** Memos had no ids and agents' work did not point back to Dinis's words (fixed in part: guidance/index.json). What else lacks a backlink?
3. **"The designer did not follow guidance it was given."** Where was the gap in its prompt or ROLE, so Dinis never has to tell an agent twice?

## Rules
- At most one or two second stories every four hours, proposed by the maestros; Dinis gets a debrief at the end.
